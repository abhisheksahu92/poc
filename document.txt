	•	In images (like .png), the origin (0,0) is at the top-left corner.
	•	Also, the model’s coordinate range (like 0–10 or 0–20) doesn’t match the image’s actual size (like 964×3119 or 1536×1152 pixels).
Because of these differences, the same polygon ends up in the wrong place when drawn directly on the image.
